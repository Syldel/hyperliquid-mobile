Hyperliquid charges one tier per account, the same for every asset. It is what userFees reports, and what the figures above come from.

Checking a line

Each fill shows the fee it actually paid. Divided by its notional — fee / (price × size) — it should land on one of the two rates above.

Why HIP-3 lines differ

On a market deployed by a third party, the rate is the tier multiplied by that deployer's fee scale, and by 0.1 again if the market runs in growth mode. Both sides are scaled, taker and maker.

Measured on real fills rather than deduced: every xyz market runs at a scale of 1.0 with growth mode on, which bills 0.0090% taker and 0.0030% maker — five times below the tier above, not above it.

This screen shows the tier, not the rate of each market: that would take one request per venue in the list, and your referral discount is not readable here. The lines concerned carry a HIP-3 badge instead.
